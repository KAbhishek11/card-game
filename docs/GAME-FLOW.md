# Gupt Sabha game flow
Working title; all characters are fictional people in an imagined Indian court. Three cosmetic themes share identical rules.

## Enter
Create room → enter display name → choose art theme and standard or optional roles → share link/code → 5–10 friends join → each confirms readiness → host starts.

## Learn your identity
Each device receives only its own role and permitted knowledge. Tap to reveal; hide before handing over a phone. Everyone acknowledges their role before the first proposal.

## Play
1. Rotating leader proposes the required number of members. Discuss aloud in person or on your own voice call.
2. Everyone secretly approves or rejects. Once all votes arrive, show the complete public team vote. A strict majority approves; a tie rejects.
3. An approved team privately chooses Support or Sabotage. Loyal players can only Support. Show only aggregate sabotage counts, never individual quest cards.
4. Three successful expeditions trigger the final accusation. Three failed expeditions or five consecutive rejected teams give the conspirators victory. The rejection count resets on an approved team.
5. The Blade makes one accusation: identifying the Seer overturns three successes; otherwise the loyal court wins.
6. Reveal all roles and results. Host can return everyone to the lobby for a fresh game.

## Roles
- Tara, the Seer (loyal): sees conspirators except the Veiled One; survive the final accusation.
- Veer, the Blade (conspirator): knows other conspirators except the Outcast; makes the final accusation.
- Court Ally (loyal): no special information.
- Conspirator: knows fellow conspirators except the Outcast.
- Dev, the Keeper (optional loyal): sees Seer and Impostor together without knowing which is which.
- Maya, the Impostor (optional conspirator): appears to the Keeper alongside the Seer.
- Nalin, the Veiled One (optional conspirator): hidden from the Seer.
- Ketu, the Outcast (optional conspirator): neither knows nor is known to fellow conspirators; still visible to the Seer.

## Counts and expedition sizes
| Players | Loyal / conspirators | Expedition team sizes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 3 / 2 | 2, 3, 2, 3, 3 |
| 6 | 4 / 2 | 2, 3, 4, 3, 4 |
| 7 | 4 / 3 | 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 |
| 8 | 5 / 3 | 3, 4, 4, 5, 5 |
| 9 | 6 / 3 | 3, 4, 4, 5, 5 |
| 10 | 6 / 4 | 3, 4, 4, 5, 5 |
Only expedition four at 7–10 players needs two sabotage cards to fail.

## Recovery and boundaries
Reloading restores a seat through a private session token. Disconnected players retain their seats; no one silently substitutes for them. After a host has disconnected for 60 seconds, another connected player can claim hosting. A lobby host may remove disconnected seats; during a game they may return the room to the lobby with an explicit confirmation. Rooms persist on local disk and expire after 24 hours of inactivity. A single server instance must own the room store. Separate browsers/devices are required for distinct human players; separate tabs use separate session storage.
